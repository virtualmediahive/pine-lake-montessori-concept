The prepared environment

Five areas, one classroom.

Hands-on, self-correcting Montessori materials let children learn by doing, touching and experiencing. Hover each area to look around.

  • Practical life

    Everyday skills that build independence.

    • Dressing and self-care
    • Preparing snacks
    • Caring for the classroom

  • Sensorial

    Materials that sharpen the senses.

    • Shapes, sizes and colours
    • Textures, tastes and smells
    • Length, volume and sequence

  • Language

    From first words to writing journals.

    • Daily story times
    • Picture and sequence cards
    • Reading and writing at their own pace

  • Math

    Counting, measuring and real understanding.

    • Concrete materials first
    • Counting and rhythm every day
    • Progress without a fixed timeline

  • Culture & French

    The world, brought into the classroom.

    • Geography, science, history, art
    • Traditions, food and songs from many countries
    • French games, plays and conversation
